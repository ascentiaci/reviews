The instructor is knowledgeable, supportive, and always willing to answer questions, creating a positive and encouraging learning environment. The practical sessions have helped me build confidence in assisting with dental procedures, infection control, patient care, and chairside techniques.

I really appreciate the quality of the learning resources provided throughout the program. This course has strengthened my skills, increased my confidence, and prepared me for a successful career as a dental assistant in Canada. I would highly recommend this program to anyone looking to enter the dental field and build a rewarding future.
