I had such a great experience with the massage students at Ascentia! The facility is clean, welcoming, and massage rooms are relaxing. The staff and students are incredibly kind and professional too.

The graduating students do an amazing job! Both my treatments provided relaxation and real relief from muscle tension and pain. The students listen, are receptive to feedback and genuinely care about their clients and what they’re learning.

I would highly recommend Ascentia Career Institute and using the students Spa program reduced rates while you can!
