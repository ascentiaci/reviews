I had a wonderful experience taking the Holistic Spa & Wellness Massage course at Essentia Career Institute. The course gave me the knowledge, hands-on skills and confidence I needed to get my own mobile massage business off the ground, which has been a success! I didn’t have the time or resources to commit to a full RMT program, so this was the perfect option for me. The amount of knowledge and practical training I gained in such a short time was impressive.

Essentia is a welcoming, friendly and inclusive learning environment, and no previous experience is needed to get started. The administrative team is incredibly supportive, approachable and attentive, offering a very personal, one-on-one experience. The tuition is very reasonable, and they can also help students explore government funding options, which is worth looking into if finances are a barrier to pursuing your goals.

Whether you’re looking to explore the world of massage, develop new skills or take the first steps toward starting your own business, I highly recommend Essentia Career Institute. It was a great stepping stone for me, and I’m grateful for the opportunity it gave me to turn a new interest into a successful business!
