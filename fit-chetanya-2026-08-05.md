I came into this course with no healthcare background, but I wanted to qualify for a TEER 3 job in the healthcare sector. That's when I found Ascentia Career Institute and enrolled in their Chairside Dental Assistant / Dental Assistant Level I / Licensed Dental Assistant (Limited) program.

The program is about six months long, and the biggest challenge for me was staying consistent with my studies while balancing night shifts. If you make your education a priority and communicate with the instructors, they are very understanding. There were times when I felt completely overwhelmed and even considered dropping out, but the staff took the time to counsel me, encourage me, and help me get back on track. Their support made a huge difference.

One thing I really appreciated was that there were no inflated tuition fees for international students. The tuition was the same for everyone, which I found very fair. At the time, I wasn't aware that student financial aid was available, but even without it, the program was still accessible.

The institute provides all the books and study materials, so you don't have to worry about purchasing additional resources. My instructor was highly experienced and provided one-on-one guidance in both theory and clinical skills throughout the program. Even when I came to class exhausted after working night shifts, they motivated me, held me accountable, and pushed me to do my best. Looking back, I truly appreciate that because sometimes you need someone who believes in you more than you believe in yourself.

Within five months, I had gained valuable knowledge, earned multiple certificates, completed my coursework, and was prepared for my practicum. One of the best parts is that Ascentia arranges practicums in your own city whenever possible, making the transition into the workforce much easier. I'm about to begin my practicum next week, and I already feel much more confident about my future.

They also provide support with resumes, job searching, and career guidance after the course, which adds tremendous value.

Overall, I highly recommend Ascentia Career Institute to anyone looking to start a career in dental assisting or enter the healthcare field. If you're willing to put in the effort, the instructors and staff will support you every step of the way.
